# **GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT (GDD)**

## **Project Name: Game Design Document: The Shining Ones (Working Title)**

### **1. PROJECT OVERVIEW**

**1.1 High-Concept Summary:** A dual-reality RPG where players navigate a bleak, monochrome noir city to investigate a global sleeping sickness, then pilot a ship across the collective unconscious to battle hyper-saturated nightmares in classic turn-based combat.

**1.2 Genre:** Investigation / Noir Mystery (Real World Simulation) + JRPG / Conditional Time Battle (Dreamscape) + Ship Management/Survival (Collective Unconscious).

**1.3 Target Audience:** Mature players seeking psychological thrillers, tactical JRPG combat, and narrative-heavy simulations. Fans of games blending bleak reality with surreal dungeon crawling. The demographic appreciates high-stakes resource management, permadeath mechanics, and deep party building.

**1.4 Core Pillars:**

* **Profound Isolation vs. Dream Camaraderie:** You are entirely alone in the waking world, relying only on encrypted texts, but fight side-by-side with avatars in the dreamscape.
* **Paranoia and Anonymity:** Allies cannot share real names until maximum trust is earned. The threat could be anyone.
* **High-Stakes Triage:** Combat is deadly. Falling in the dreamscape means permanent coma for allies, and a hard Game Over if the player character falls.
* **Visual Dissonance:** Stark black-and-white noir vs. blinding, chaotic color.

### **2. STORY \& NARRATIVE**

**2.1 Premise:** A mysterious sleeping sickness is sweeping the globe. A huge portion of humanity cannot be awoken, while others are struck with total insomnia, going mad in the streets. The sickness is not natural; a cult known as "The Dreamers" has captured the mythic entity The Sandman, using his power to force humanity to "ascend" into the dream world. The player is a "Shining One", capable of lucid dreaming and entering the collective unconscious to fight back.

**2.2 Setting the Worlds:**

* **The Waking World (Quarantined Apartment):** A lightly furnished monochrome studio apartment bathed in the amber glow of a monitor. The player is strictly quarantined. The outside world is heard through the walls (rain, sirens, violent insomniacs) and seen only through clinical TV news broadcasts. Clicking physical objects like the computer, phone, or bed triggers their respective functions.
* **The Collective Unconscious:** A pitch-black sea of liquid thought that reacts to the ambient mood of the waking world.
* **The Library (Hub):** A vast library floating on the pitch-black sea, marked by a fixed amber lighthouse. The only safe haven. Smells of old parchment and rotting sea salt.
* **The Dreamscapes:** Hyper-saturated, colorful islands born from the fractured psyches of comatose patients.

**2.3 Characters / Factions:**

* **The Dreamers (Cult):** An organized, fanatic cult piloting armored dreadnoughts on the dark sea. Their leader wields the Sandman's stolen cloak and hourglass, manipulating time itself. He is untouchable in the real world and must be hunted in the dreamscape.
* **The Vultures:** Independent rival Shining Ones who have abandoned the mission, choosing to harvest power by preying on weaker ships.
* **The Party (Shining Ones - The 12 Archetypes):** A finite cast of 12 recruit-able allies corresponding to the 12 base Jungian archetypes. Known initially only by encrypted aliases, they suffer profound isolation in the waking world. In the dreamscape, they manifest as their idealized constellation archetypes.

  * **1. The Hero (Player Character):** A solitary investigator. *Strength:* Adaptable and driven. *Flaw:* Pathological isolationist. *Combat:* A balanced DPS/Support who uses a physical Charm to anchor to reality.
  * **2. The Caregiver (Brick):** *Waking World:* A teenage girl in a group home, fiercely protecting the younger kids. *Quirk:* Constantly checks in on others' well-being. *Flaw:* Self-sacrificing to a destructive fault. *Combat:* Manifests as a towering man with a massive sword. A slow, frontline tank who absorbs heavy physical damage.
  * **3. The Magician (Cipher):** *Waking World:* An elderly, bedridden former cryptographer whose body is failing. *Quirk:* Speaks in riddles and codes over text. *Flaw:* Arrogant and deeply frustrated by physical helplessness. *Combat:* A floating, cloaked figure of neon geometry. A glass-cannon "mage" expending massive Resolve for screen-clearing AoE attacks.
  * **4. The Creator (Weaver):** *Waking World:* A creatively bankrupt data-entry clerk living in their car. *Quirk:* Compulsively taps rhythmic patterns. *Flaw:* Deeply insecure and avoids confrontation. *Combat:* A multi-armed artisan dripping vibrant paint. A setup/control specialist who places traps and barriers on the CTB timeline.
  * **5. The Ruler (Sovereign):** *Waking World:* A disgraced, bankrupt middle-manager stripped of all authority. *Quirk:* Uses overly formal, corporate language in encrypted comms. *Flaw:* Controlling and terrified of irrelevance. *Combat:* A regal knight in blinding golden armor. A Commander/Support who cannot attack directly, but buffs stats and physically pulls allies up the timeline.
  * **6. The Explorer (Pathfinder):** *Waking World:* A severe agoraphobic who hasn't left their apartment in a decade. *Quirk:* Obsessed with digital maps and weather forecasts. *Flaw:* Flighty and terrified of physical reality. *Combat:* A sleek, agile rogue adorned in glowing feathers. Features the highest evasion/speed, bypassing enemy armor and manipulating overworld stealth.
  * **7. The Outlaw (Vandal):** *Waking World:* A strict, anxious, by-the-books paralegal. *Quirk:* Aggressively corrects grammar in chat. *Flaw:* Repressed and passive-aggressive. *Combat:* A punk-aesthetic gunslinger radiating chaotic energy. The primary debuffer who inflicts status ailments, shatters armor, and delays nightmare turns.
  * **8. The Lover (Muse):** *Waking World:* A bitter widower who actively pushes everyone away to avoid future loss. *Quirk:* Sends blunt, single-word replies until trust is earned. *Flaw:* Fear of attachment. *Combat:* A beautiful, siren-like figure wielding a crystalline harp. A dedicated healer and Resolve battery who sacrifices their own HP to heal others.
  * **9. The Jester (Harlequin):** *Waking World:* A severely burnt-out crisis counselor. *Quirk:* Uses inappropriate gallows humor in bleak situations. *Flaw:* Masks profound pain with erratic jokes. *Combat:* A masked trickster thriving on chaos. Relies on high-risk RNG mechanics—attacks may deal 1 damage or execute an elite, and can randomize the turn order.
  * **10. The Sage (Oracle):** *Waking World:* A high school dropout and paranoid conspiracy theorist. *Quirk:* Connects wildly unrelated digital footprints. *Flaw:* Delusional and highly distrustful. *Combat:* A blindfolded ascetic floating above the ground. Scans enemies to permanently reveal weaknesses, nullifies hazards, and foresees Leviathan attacks.
  * **11. The Innocent (Aegis):** *Waking World:* A cynical, hardened ex-convict who lost their childhood to the system. *Quirk:* Digitally hoards pictures of small, shiny trinkets. *Flaw:* Jaded and slow to trust. *Combat:* A radiant, ethereal child in a glowing sphere. A pure damage mitigator, immune to ailments and capable of rendering an ally completely invulnerable.
  * **12. The Orphan (Legion):** *Waking World:* A wealthy, highly privileged, and narcissistic socialite feeling utterly empty. *Quirk:* Complains about trivial inconveniences during global crises. *Flaw:* Out of touch and self-centered. *Combat:* A faceless, shifting amalgam of ordinary people. A "Blue Mage" who must survive enemy attacks to permanently learn and mimic nightmare abilities.

**2.4 The Library Inhabitants:**

* **The Archivist:** Resides in the central hall. The sharp tang of melting metal surrounds their forge. They hammer jagged shards of scrap into usable concepts. They upgrade Charms and unlock new nodes on the constellation skill trees.
* **The Bartender:** Occupies the unlocked rustic taproom. You hear the clinking of heavy glass before you see him. The room smells of stale ale and ancient woodsmoke. He sells Emotional Salves, trades rumors for conceptual scrap, and provides hints about Leviathan weaknesses.
* **The Shipwright:** Works in the flooded lower vaults. Cold seawater sloshes against stone floors. The air hangs heavy with the scent of hot tar and rusted iron. She takes scrap to fortify the hull and reinforce the engines against the dark water.
* **The Navigator:** Sits in the highest tower. Wind howls through broken glass. The room reeks of burning wax and ozone. He charts the pitch-black sea, trades waking world data for hidden coordinates, points the way to rare elite islands, and tracks Cult movements.
* **The Hound:** A massive black dog named Nyx sleeps by the central hearth. Its thick fur smells of fresh rain and damp earth. It watches the main doors and never barks. Players can sit beside the hound for a quiet moment to restore a fraction of Resolve before a deep dive.

### **3. GAMEPLAY \& MECHANICS**

**3.1 The Core Loop:**

1. **The Waking Shift:** Move through the physical apartment. Engage with the computer, phone, or television to trigger narrative events.
2. **Time Management:** Performing complex hacks, researching anchors, or maintaining long text conversations consumes a "Shift". Players must prioritize limited actions before exhaustion forces them to sleep.
3. **Navigate (The Sea Overworld):** Pilot the ship across the dark water. Avoid hostile vision cones.
4. **Infiltrate (Dreamscape):** Explore procedural roguelike dungeons. Balance stealth, exploration, and turn-based combat. Once the boss falls, the island's collapse/escape is handled narratively as the patient wakes up, returning the crew to the ship.

**3.2 Waking World Mechanics (The Apartment Simulation):**

* **The Shift System:** Time dictates progress. Deep investigations or lengthy communications eat up discrete chunks of the day.
* **Narrative Interface (Ink & Text Animator):** Clicking physical objects in the room transitions the gameplay into text-driven narrative menus powered by **Ink by Inkle**. Text is rendered with **Text Animator for Unity (Febucci)** featuring mechanical typewriter pacing, corrupted glitching, and per-letter mechanical keyboard foley. The investigation unfolds through descriptive prose and branching dialogue trees.
* **Dice-Driven Resolution:** Outcomes for critical waking world actions rely on 3D wireframe dice rolls modified by the player's core stats (Intuition, Grit, Logic, Empathy). A failed dice roll consumes a Shift without yielding progress.
* **Digital Anchors:** Anchors are deep connections to the sleeper. These manifest as voicemails, redacted police reports, magazine covers or strange code. The core puzzle involves using the desktop OS to uncover a deep secret. This secret becomes the navigational marker required to locate the patient's island in the dreamscape.

**3.3 The Ship & Navigation (The Sea Overworld):**

* **Piloting & Stealth (Sensor Toolkit 2 & Procedural FOV Cones):** Manage hull integrity. The pitch-black sea functions as an overworld where players navigate stealthily using an amber Sonar Ring while evading dynamic enemy vision cones. Vision cones are rendered as procedural polygon mesh fans that physically hug the dark water surface and dynamically clip around island landmasses. If the ship penetrates a cone, enemy patrols (using Sensor Toolkit 2 context steering) turn to pursue and engage.
* **Deck Combat (Grinding):** When Vultures attack, the perspective swaps to standard JRPG combat on the ship's deck. This provides a repeatable loop to grind for parts, experience, and conceptual scrap.

**3.4 Dreamscape Combat System (Conditional Time Battle - CTB):**

* **Timeline:** Displayed as a vertical, glowing amber paint smear on the left side of the screen. Portraits move down the smear.
* **Resolve (Resource):** Functions like MP. Generated by attacking, taking damage, and defending. All special abilities consume Resolve.
* **Stabilization \& Permadeath:** When an ally hits 0 HP, the player must sacrifice their entire turn and drain their *entire* Resolve meter to stabilize them. Failure means the ally slips into an eternal coma. If the Player Character hits 0 HP, a hard Game Over triggers and the player must reload their last save.
* **In-World UI:** Action menus (Fight, Defend, Item) are projected as glowing amber graffiti directly onto the 3D environment next to the character.

### **4. ART \& AUDIO DIRECTION**

**4.1 Visual Style:**

* **Real World:** Gritty noir simulation, 100% black and white, with a *single* accent color: Amber.
* **The Sea \& Library:** Pitch black water, glowing amber dials, towering avatars.
* **The Dreamscape:** Blinding, hyper-saturated colors.
* **Active Camera (Cinemachine):** Highly dynamic 3D camera. Snaps to characters on their turn, restless idles, and violent target shifts, all operating behind the static, 2D noir UI overlay.

**4.2 Audio Design:**

* **Real World:** Heavy mechanical clacks of a keyboard, static radio drone, frantic scratching/screaming from insomniacs outside the apartment walls.
* **Combat Crisis:** When an ally falls, battle music is drowned out by a low electric hum and a heavy heartbeat.

### **5. ENEMIES \& BOSSES**

**5.1 Enemy Philosophy:** Invasive psychological tumors dressed in bright colors.

* **The Stagnant (Regret):** Brass and stained glass. Push party down the CTB timeline.
* **The Smothering (Control):** Silk mannequins. Drain Resolve.
* **The Swarm (Paranoia):** Floating geometric eyes. Massive action economy.

**5.2 Sea Hazards \& Bosses:**

* **Leviathans:** Massive optional bosses roaming the collective unconscious. Each manifests generalized fears, wants or needs. These are multi-phase or multi-part endurance battles. Leviathans cannot simply be pummeled to death. They require solving combat puzzles like destroying a regenerating appendage to progress. They unleash area-of-effect attacks that simultaneously damage all party members and the ship's Hull Integrity.
* **The Cult Leader:** The final boss. Uses the Sandman's hourglass to actively manipulate the UI timeline and the cloak to enforce invulnerability until shattered by the correct Anchor.

### **6. PROGRESSION \& ECONOMY**

**6.1 Core Attributes \& Leveling:**

* **The Four Pillars:** Characters are defined by four core stats: Intuition, Grit, Logic, and Empathy. These stats bridge the physical apartment and the collective unconscious. Earning experience in the dreamscape increases these stats, improving both JRPG combat prowess and waking-world narrative dice checks.

**6.2 Dreamscape Loadouts (Gear \& Abilities):**

* **The Player Character's Arsenal:** The protagonist utilizes three equipable slots: one Charm (weapon) and two Accessories. The Charm anchors the character to reality and its special attacks consume Resolve. Charms are upgraded at the Library hub using Conceptual Scrap. Accessories provide passive benefits and are found as dungeon loot or rewards for waking-world investigations.
* **Party Member Progression:** Ally loadouts evolve entirely through bespoke skill trees rather than manual gear management. Players allocate points as allies level up to unlock abilities and passive traits. All unlocked special abilities consume Resolve.

**6.3 Itemization \& Loot:**

* **Waking World:** There are no items to manage in the waking world. Uncovering a digital Anchor manifests it as a physical object in the dreamscape.
* **Accessories:** Equipable items that modify character builds and provide passive benefits.
* **Emotional Salves:** Basic healing potions purchased at the Library once the shop is restored.

### **6.4 The Constellation Skill Trees**

* **Authoring Pipeline (xNode):** The 12 constellation trees are authored visually in the Unity Editor using **xNode** graph canvases. Designers connect star nodes, set attribute modifiers, and assign active/passive abilities, which compile cleanly into the atomic JSON progression system.
* **In-Game Visual Interface:** Accessing an ally's skill tree via the pause menu freezes the dreamscape. The background shifts to a pitch-black night sky. The skill tree projects as a sprawling constellation representing the character's specific Jungian archetype (e.g., a shield for The Caregiver, a crown for The Ruler). Rendered on a screen-space UGUI canvas combining star sprites with glowing amber `LineRenderer` paths and DOTween pulse animations.
* **Skill Points (SP):** Surviving battles and clearing Patient Islands grants experience points. Leveling up awards SP. The player manually allocates SP to connect stars and dictate the ally's combat loadout. This entirely replaces traditional gear management.
* **Node Architecture:**

  * **Attribute Stars:** Flat permanent stat bumps to the four core pillars (Intuition, Grit, Logic and Empathy). These raise the baseline effectiveness for the character on the timeline.
  * **Active Stars:** Unlocks new tactical actions that project as amber graffiti during combat. Every active node inherently consumes Resolve to execute.
  * **Passive Stars:** Conditional combat modifiers. These include mechanics like generating bonus Resolve when struck, auto-shifting position on the timeline or increasing defense against specific nightmares.
* **Branching Specialization:** Tracing lines between stars forces deliberate pathing. A player might route a defensive ally heavily into Grit and Logic branches. This locks them out of the Empathy support nodes on the opposite side of the constellation.
* **The Trust Lock (Ultimate Ability):** The final apex star remains dark. It cannot be unlocked with SP. The star only ignites when the player maximizes the waking world Trust Score through encrypted comms and Library interactions. This reveals the ally's true name and activates their ultimate ability.

### **6.5 The Conceptual Scrap Economy**

* **Visual \& Audio Design:** Conceptual Scrap manifests as jagged, heavy shards of glass that glow with a dull amber light. When collected, it chimes with the heavy, satisfying clatter of metal coins dropping into a tin cup, contrasting with the fluid nature of the dreamscape.
* **Harvesting (Inflow):**

  * **Flotsam (Low Yield):** Skimming scattered shards directly off the pitch-black sea while navigating the overworld.
  * **Combat Drops (Medium Yield):** Shattering nightmares during JRPG combat on Patient Islands or defending the deck from Vultures.
  * **Leviathan Scales (High Yield):** Surviving a Leviathan encounter or successfully breaking one of its appendages yields massive, high-density scrap clusters.
  * **Waking World Breakthroughs:** Successfully resolving difficult waking-world investigations (dice rolls) can grant a cache of scrap, representing a moment of mental clarity that physically manifests during the next dive.
* **Expenditure (Outflow) at The Library:**

  * Players spend scrap at a specific wing of the Library, managed by the "Archivist," an NPC who hammers the shards into usable concepts.
  * **Ship Upgrades (Survival):** Reinforcing Hull Integrity (HP) to survive overworld ambushes, or upgrading the Sonar Ring and Engines to make evading vision cones easier.
  * **Charm Forging (Combat):** Upgrading the Player Character's physical tether to reality. This increases their base damage in JRPG combat and unlocks advanced, high-cost Resolve attacks.
  * **Library Restoration (Meta-Progression):** At the start of the game, the Library hub and Lighthouse are decaying and falling apart. Players can spend massive amounts of scrap to physically restore the architecture of the Library. This acts as the game's core meta-progression loop. Restoring wings of the Library unlocks new upgrade options, expands the inventory of available Accessories, and provides permanent, party-wide buffs for all future dives. While XP levels individual Shining Ones, restoring the Library builds up the entire team and fortifies their foothold in the collective unconscious.

### **7. LEVEL DESIGN \& WORLD BUILDING**

**7.1 Real World Zones:**

* **The Quarantined Apartment:** The only real-world location. A claustrophobic physical space where players click on objects to navigate the narrative menus.

**7.2 Dreamscape Topography:**

* **The Pitch-Black Sea:** An open-water stealth/survival overworld.
* **The Library:** A fixed sanctuary hub.
* **Patient Islands:** Procedurally generated roguelike JRPG dungeons reflecting specific traumas featuring stealth, exploration, and combat.

### **7.3 Patient Island Generation (In-Editor Curated Pipeline):**

* **Generation Workflow (Gaia & GeNa):** Rather than unpredictable runtime procedural generation that risks navigation bugs, broken seeds, and framerate hitching, Patient Islands utilize an **offline curated procedural workflow**:
  * **Gaia:** Stamps out the island's macro landmasses, sea cliffs, and coastal shelves directly in the Unity Editor.
  * **GeNa & Custom Spawners:** Automatically assembles modular Synty room chunks, filing-cabinet corridors, and hazard zones along procedural splines.
  * **Curated Handcrafting:** Designers hand-tune enemy patrol routes, memory fragment lock placements, and the dramatic architecture of the *Core* boss arena. The level is baked and loaded seamlessly at runtime with zero hitching.
* **The Biome Foundation:** The digital Anchor dictates the environment. The secret uncovered on the OS computer forms the foundation of the island. A redacted police report spawns a warped precinct maze of rusted filing cabinets and flickering fluorescents.
* **Structural Anatomy:** Islands follow a three-act layout:
  * **The Periphery:** Outer stealth zones where players navigate blind spots to avoid Vulture and nightmare patrols.
  * **The Suppression Wards:** Deeper heavy combat arenas where progression doors require specific memory fragments to unlock.
  * **The Core:** A handcrafted, dramatic epicenter housing the comatose patient's trauma boss.
* **Psychological Hazards:** Environmental traps scale with the patient's specific neuroses. Paranoia manifests as glass floors that shatter loudly to alert enemies. Regret creates spatial loops requiring the player to defeat a specific Stagnant enemy to break the cycle.
* **Loot Distribution:** Standard chests hold Emotional Salves for basic healing. Rare Accessories sit behind optional elite encounters representing deeply repressed memories.

### **8. TECHNICAL & SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS**

**8.1 Engine & Core Frameworks:**
* **Engine:** Unity 6 (`6000.6.4f1`) with Universal Render Pipeline (URP).
* **Cinemachine:** Dynamic 3D combat camera snapping, idle breathing, and fixed apartment inspection framing.
* **Dialogue & Narrative Engine:** **Ink by Inkle** (`com.inkle.ink-unity-integration`). Manages visual novel text trees, encrypted SMS threads, choice branch states, and binds directly to C# external functions for attribute-modified dice resolution.
* **Story Compilation & Linting CLI:** **Inklecate CLI** (`v1.2.1`). Enables automated out-of-engine validation, syntax linting, and rapid batch compilation of `.ink` scripts into `.json` without triggering Unity domain reloads.
* **Narrative Typography & FX Engine:** **Text Animator for Unity (Febucci)**. Integrates directly with TextMeshPro and Ink for mechanical typewriter pacing, erratic panic shakes, corrupted data glitches, and per-letter foley triggers.
* **Motion & Tweening Engine:** **DOTween (Free)** (`com.madstrawberry.dotween`). Chained elastic tweens for CTB timeline portrait sliding, Resolve vial fluid slosh, UI punch scales, and camera letterbox transitions.
* **Skill Tree Visual Graph Editor:** **xNode** (`com.github.siccity.xnode`). Custom in-editor visual node canvas for authoring, balancing, and exporting the 12 Constellation Skill Trees.
* **Perception & Stealth Sensing:** **Sensor Toolkit 2**. Powers 3D line-of-sight cone sensors for patrol vessels and radial range sensing for the player's Sonar Ring.
* **Combat System Engine:** **Ability & Combat Toolkit (ACT)**. Powers the turn-based Conditional Time Battle (CTB) logic, status effects, cooldowns, and ability execution.
* **Save & Persistence Architecture (Atomic JSON):**
  * **Format:** Strongly-typed, human-readable **JSON** (via Unity `JsonUtility` / `Newtonsoft.Json`). Avoids bloated black-box relational databases (SQLite) and platform-specific native binaries.
  * **Atomic File Writes:** State writes to an ephemeral `save_slot_XX.json.tmp` buffer before atomically replacing the active save file, preventing corruption during abrupt shutdowns or hard Game Overs.
  * **Ink State Integration:** Directly serializes Ink's active dialogue flow and variable table using `story.state.ToJson()`.
  * **Serialized Domains:**
    * *Player State:* Four core pillars (Intuition, Grit, Logic, Empathy), equipped Charm and Accessories, Conceptual Scrap balance.
    * *Party State:* Status of the 12 Archetypes (Alive, Comatose, Recruited), Trust Scores (0–100), unlocked Constellation skill nodes.
    * *Meta-Progression:* Restored Library wings, Archivist forge levels, Ship Hull Integrity, and Sonar Ring tiers.
    * *Investigation State:* Shift clock meter, unlocked digital Anchor files, and discovered patient coordinates.
  * **Performance & Debugging:** <2ms save/load overhead; files can be directly diffed in Git or edited in plain text for testing edge-case quest branches.
* **Development Automation:** Unity MCP (`unity-mcp` via local relay) for automated editor tooling, test execution, and scene inspection.

**8.2 Target Platforms:**
* PC stands as the primary platform, mirroring the in-game desktop OS.
* Secondary targets include current-generation consoles.

**8.3 Control Scheme:**
* **Mouse:** Click-to-move mechanics for navigation. Right-clicking triggers context menus for interacting with the OS, charting ship courses, and selecting combat actions.
* **Controller:** Full gamepad support. The left stick handles direct character movement. Face buttons map to right-click context menus for seamless UI interaction.

### **9. UI/UX ARCHITECTURE**

**9.1 The Waking World (Apartment & Desktop OS):**
* **The Physical Room:** Zero HUD. The screen is completely clean. The environment acts as the interface. Right-clicking an interactive object snaps the camera into a fixed perspective.
* **The Desktop OS:** Pure function. High-contrast black and white with amber accents. Powered by **All In 1 Sprite Shader** with built-in scanlines, CRT screen curvature, phosphor glow, and subtle terminal glitching.
* **The Shift Clock:** A digital readout anchored in the corner of the monitor. It ticks down only when an investigation or conversation resolves. It builds passive psychological pressure.
* **Encrypted Comms:** Text threads on the cell phone interface mimic old SMS layouts, driven dynamically through Ink narrative branches.

**9.2 The Sea Overworld (Navigation & Stealth):**
* **The Helm:** The UI frames the edges of the screen lightly.
* **Sonar Ring & Perception:** An amber billboard ring projected directly onto the pitch-black water around the ship, powered by radial range sensors to reveal submerged terrain and enemy sound profiles.
* **Vision Cones (Procedural FOV Mesh Fan):** Enemy patrol vessels and Leviathans project true dynamic vision cones using raycast-based procedural polygon mesh fans that physically hug the dark water surface and dynamically cut/clip around islands and sea rocks. Materials glow dull amber during idle sweeps and flare into jagged crimson when the player's vessel penetrates the beam.
* **Hull Integrity:** A wireframe schematic of the ship anchored to the bottom right. Damage renders sections jagged and red.
* **The Lighthouse Tuner:** Analog dials dominate the navigation menu. Players rotate them with the mouse wheel or analog stick to match frequency waves and chart a safe return to the Library.

**9.3 Dreamscape Combat (Conditional Time Battle):**
* **The Timeline:** A thick vertical smear of glowing amber paint on the left margin. Character portraits and **POLYGON Icons** slide down the paint track as turns approach.
* **Action Graffiti (Billboards):** Right-clicking targets in combat projects the action menu directly onto the 3D environment. The words FIGHT, DEFEND and ITEM bleed into the air next to the character as camera-facing unlit amber emissive billboards.
* **Resource Tracking:** Health points are stark white numbers floating above heads. The Resolve meter sits at the bottom center. It mimics a glass vial filling with liquid amber. Taking or dealing damage causes the fluid to violently slosh.
* **The Stabilization Prompt:** When an ally falls the UI aggressively shifts. The hyper-saturated colors drain to a muddy gray. A heavy heartbeat visual dominates the center screen. The prompt to sacrifice all Resolve flashes urgently over the downed ally.

**9.4 Menus & Progression (The Pause Screen):**
* **Waking Access:** Trust scores, character profiles and acquired Anchors are reviewed via the clunky cell phone interface or a physical notebook on the desk.
* **Dreamscape Access:** Pausing in a dungeon brings up the Mind's Eye screen. It freezes the hyper-saturated environment and applies a heavy monochrome filter. Clean amber data blocks overlay the screen for upgrading skill trees, equipping Charms and managing Accessories.
* **Constellation Skill Trees (xNode + UGUI & LineRenderer):** Visually authored and balanced via **xNode** editor graphs. In-game, rendered as 12 unique constellation graphs on a pitch-black starry background, where star nodes are styled sprites connected by glowing amber DOTween-animated `LineRenderer` paths, avoiding heavy vector plugins.

**9.5 Dice Rolls & Narrative Resolution (Visual Novel Style):**
* **The Transition:** Clicking a critical interactive object locks the camera. Black letterboxing frames the top and bottom of the screen. A translucent black text box anchors the lower third.
* **The Text Interface:** The heavy clack of a mechanical keyboard accompanies the text as Ink populates the box. The internal monologue of the protagonist and ambient environmental details drive the scene.
* **The Prompt:** The text box presents a branching choice. The action requires a stat check. The required attribute highlights in stark amber next to the dialogue option.
* **The Execution:** The player clicks their choice. A 3D wireframe die materializes directly over the 2D desktop OS interface. It spins rapidly with the sharp rattle of plastic tumbling across a wooden desk.
* **The Resolution:** The die locks into place. The number flashes amber for success or dull grey for failure. Ink routes immediately to the consequence branch.

### **10. THE OPENING SEQUENCE (First 10 Minutes)**

**Phase 1: In Media Res (Minutes 0 to 3)**  
The game opens directly into combat. Blinding neon colors bleed across the screen (Quibli anime shaders). The setting is a fractured stained glass cathedral floating on black water. The player controls their towering avatar and their first ally (Brick). The tutorial introduces the amber timeline. The player selects an attack. Brick follows up with a heavy physical strike. The enemy retaliates. Brick takes lethal damage. The combat music cuts out completely. A heavy heartbeat thumps through the audio track. The hyper-saturated colors drain to a muddy grey. The stabilization prompt appears. The player sacrifices all Resolve to save Brick. The stabilization succeeds but drains the avatar completely. A blinding white flash consumes the screen.

**Phase 2: The Snap (Minutes 3 to 5)**  
Absolute silence. The screen remains black. A single amber terminal cursor blinks in the center. The camera pulls back slowly. The player stares at a curved CRT monitor inside a cramped studio apartment. The visual dissonance hits immediately. The room is stark monochrome (POLYGON City + B&W post-process). The low drone of a cheap refrigerator fills the room. Sirens wail faintly through the thin walls. The player clicks to stand up. The physical room acts as the menu.

**Phase 3: The Desk (Minutes 5 to 8)**  
The player clicks the desktop computer. The camera snaps to a fixed perspective. The visual novel interface locks in. A heavy mechanical clatter sounds as Ink text populates the lower box. An encrypted, heavily guarded message arrives from Brick. The dialogue establishes the game's core paranoia: allies refuse to share real names or details. The first dice roll triggers: Brick sends a fragmented digital Anchor file requiring an Intuition check to decrypt. A 3D wireframe die drops onto the 2D desktop and clatters loudly. The outcome dictates the next text prompt.

**Phase 4: The Metronome (Minutes 8 to 10)**  
The player backs away from the desk and clicks the television. Static buzzes sharply. A clinical news anchor details the rising global coma count. The broadcast sets the narrative stakes. ### **11.1 3D Environments & World Building**
* **The Waking World (Monochrome Noir Apartment & City):**
  * **Source Asset:** Synty **POLYGON - City Pack**.

* **The Collective Unconscious & Ships:**
  * **Source Asset:** Synty **POLYGON - Pirates Pack**.
  * **Vessels:** Customizable player vessel, Vulture raider ships, dreadnoughts, and sea wreckage.
  * **The Pitch-Black Sea:** Mesh-based stylized water plane from POLYGON Pirates configured with an obsidian/pitch-black albedo, deep refraction absorption, and an emissive amber foam/wake fringe that illuminates as vessels cut through the dark liquid thought.
  * **Overworld Perception & Stealth:** **Sensor Toolkit 2** powers line-of-sight cone sensing and radial Sonar Ring tracking. Enemy vision cones are rendered via a **Procedural FOV Mesh Fan** (Option B) that dynamically hugs the water surface and wraps/clips around island terrain, shifting from pale amber (idle patrol) to jagged crimson (detection).
* **Dreamscape Patient Islands (Biome Generation):**
  * **Offline Curated Generation:** Built using **Gaia** (island landmasses, cliff stamping, coastlines) and **GeNa** (rule-based placement of modular architectural rooms, filing-cabinet mazes, corridors, and handcrafted Core boss arenas). Generates layout variations in-editor to eliminate runtime stutter, broken seeds, and pathfinding glitches.
  * **Biome Packs:** Synty **POLYGON - Dungeons**, **Fantasy Medieval**, **Sci-Fi**, **Western / Western Frontier**, and **Farm** packs representing diverse comatose psyche islands.
  * **Stylized Rendering:** **Quibli Anime Shaders and Tools** (StylizedLit toon/cel shading, vibrant gradient ramps, rim lights, and stylized outlines) applied over low-poly meshes to generate the intense, blinding hyper-saturation of the dreamscapes.

### **11.2 Combat, Characters & UI Systems**
* **Combat Engine:** **Ability & Combat Toolkit (ACT)**. Manages turn-based timeline order, ability triggers, cooldowns, and combat state machines.
* **Character Creation & Dual-Form Pipeline (The 12 Archetypes):**
  * **Core Tool:** **Synty POLYGON - Sidekicks Character Creator** (Unity 6 URP compatible).
  * **Waking World Humans:** Crafted with everyday civilian attire (suits, office wear, hoodies, casual clothing) reflecting mundane vulnerability and psychological isolation, rendered through the grayscale noir post-processing profile.
  * **Dreamscape Avatars:** Built modularly using Sidekicks armor, fantasy gear, cloaks, and weapons to represent the 12 idealized Jungian archetypes (e.g. Brick's massive armored tank, Sovereign's regal golden commander, Vandal's punk gunslinger). Rendered with **Quibli Anime Shaders** (`StylizedLit`) for vibrant, cel-shaded saturation.
  * **Animation Rigging:** Universal Unity Humanoid (Mecanim) bone structure ensures seamless animation sharing across both mundane and dream forms using the **FREE - 32 RPG Animations** and combat motion clips.
  * **2D Portrait Generation:** Posed 3D Sidekicks models are captured in-editor with transparent backgrounds to supply 1:1 matching 2D character portraits for **Ink visual novel dialogue boxes**, encrypted SMS chat threads, and the **vertical amber CTB combat timeline**.
* **Nightmares & Enemies:** Custom kitbashed psychological foes from Synty packs + **POLYGON Boss Zombies**, **Alien Arachnoid Drone**, and **Dragons** for nightmare boss and elite manifestations.
* **Visual Effects:** Synty **Polygon Particle FX** and **100 Special Skills Effects Pack** for elemental strikes, constellation buffs, and Resolve expenditure.
* **UI & HUD Badges:** Synty **POLYGON - Icons Pack** for skill trees, item inventories, status ailments, and CTB timeline portrait markers.

* **Constellation Skill Trees:** Visually constructed using **xNode** editor graphs, rendered in-game on a screen-space UGUI canvas combining archetype star sprites with Unity's built-in `LineRenderer` with additive amber bloom shaders and DOTween node pulse animations.

* **UI & Timeline Motion:** **DOTween** powering fluid CTB portrait transitions, Resolve vial slosh animations, and UI punch scaling.
